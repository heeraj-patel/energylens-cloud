# Austin Energy heat pump water heater rebate research packet

This packet is a stopped staging draft, not an importable offer. It was captured
on October 5, 2026 while continuing Texas and California program coverage.
The [official offer page](https://austinenergy.com/energy-efficiency/rebates-incentives/residential/appliances-equipment/hp-water-heater)
describes a $1,000 rebate for a qualifying new ENERGY STAR heat pump water
heater, permanent Austin Energy electric service, an application within 90
days of purchase, and other equipment and installation conditions. Replacing
an existing heat pump water heater requires the old one to be at least ten
years old. Funding and an individual award are not guaranteed.

`capture.json` records the exact private HTML capture, SHA-256, byte count,
URL, and one-time access check. The HTML is outside Git. The Austin Energy
page does not provide a five-digit ZIP list or prove service at a household
address. `draft-review.json` therefore has an empty postal list and intentionally
cannot pass the Cloud importer; do not fill it with a ZIP inferred from an
unrelated page. A future import needs source-backed service geography,
current funding and terms, an operator access determination, and a fresh
capture. The proposed title and benefit in `proposed-copy.json` were returned
for revision by the owner and must not appear in customer UI.

No source was registered, no offer was imported, and no production or local
customer database was changed by this packet.
